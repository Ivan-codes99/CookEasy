# Purpose
The goal of this cooking web app is to help users generate recipes based on the ingredients they currently have in their kitchen stock. The app will utilize AI to suggest meals and generate recipes (or fetch from website), reducing food waste and making meal planning more convenient. Users will be able to maintain a virtual kitchen stock.

# Core Functionalities

## Ingredient Management
- Users can manually enter ingredients they have in their kitchen.
- A kitchen stock will be maintained to keep track of available ingredients.
- Users can review and update their ingredient stock at any time.

## Recipe Generation
- AI-powered meal suggestions based on the available ingredients.
- AI-powered recipe generation based on the generated meal suggestions.
- Users can view multiple meal/recipe options and select their preferred choice.

## Kitchen Stock Updates
- When a user selects a recipe, the required ingredients will be removed from their stock.
- Users will have the opportunity to review and adjust which ingredients are deducted before finalizing.
